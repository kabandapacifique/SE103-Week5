What * meant in my pattern:
The * wildcard matched any characters in the filename after data. Therefore, data*.txt matched data1.txt, data2.txt, and data3.txt.

Why I listed matches before copying:
I listed the matches first to confirm which files the wildcard would select. This helped me make sure that only the intended files would be copied.

Why wildcards are dangerous with delete commands:
Wildcards can match more files than expected if the pattern is too broad. Using a wildcard with a delete command can remove multiple files at once. I should always list and verify the matches before using a wildcard with a destructive command.
